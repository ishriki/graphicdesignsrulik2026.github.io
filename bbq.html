Chaim’s Grill

A visual identity for a new barbecue service, from logo concepts to Hebrew menus.

The brief

Chaim needed a logo and two menus for his new barbecue service. I explored three logo directions in Canva and Photoshop, using a barbecue motif and contrasting colors to reflect his preferences.

I carried the visual identity into a T-shirt application and two double-sided Hebrew menus.
