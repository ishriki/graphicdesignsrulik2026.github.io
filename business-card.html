Business card design

I.S.H Stills Photographer

A business card redesign that made contact details easier to distinguish and gave the Instagram QR code room to scan.

The brief

A stills photographer needed a business card to hand out at events and meetings. He wanted the visual identity to reflect his Instagram presence. I interviewed him on January 16, 2026, delivered the first version on January 21, and revised the card on January 26 after hearing where readers had trouble.

What readers struggled with

On the first card, readers had trouble distinguishing email from Instagram. Two nearby QR codes could appear in the camera at once. One reader preferred to text, but the WhatsApp option was difficult to find.

What changed

Clearer hierarchy

The front establishes the service through the logo, icon, and occupation title. The reverse lists phone first, then Instagram, WhatsApp, and email.

One scan target

I kept the Instagram QR code and gave it a dedicated space, addressing the scanning conflict and the visibility issues observed with the initial bright orange code.

Right-to-left layout

The reverse follows the reading direction of a Hebrew-speaking audience and gives longer contact information room to fit.

Recognizable contact icons

Icons help readers distinguish the channels and choose how to contact the photographer.
